About Me

Hello! I’m Karuna Biswas Satu, a proud graduate of Computer Science and Engineering from Daffodil International University. I completed my HSC at Savar Govt. College (2021), while my SSC (2019) and JSC (2016) were completed at Vasha Shaheed Abdul Jabbar Ansar VDP School and College. I have a solid foundation in core programming principles, including Data Structures, Algorithms, and Problem-Solving, which enables me to develop efficient software systems and intelligent applications.

My technical skills include proficiency in MS Word, Excel, and PowerPoint for documentation, data management, and presentations, along with Adobe Photoshop for creative design. My academic and technical interests range from Compiler Design to advanced Artificial Intelligence applications, including research focused on healthcare prediction using Machine Learning.

I also have experience in mobile application development using Flutter and Firebase, with a strong interest in creating intuitive, user-friendly digital experiences. Throughout my academic journey, I have worked on various projects, including Role-Based Management Systems and 3D Computer Graphics animations, applying my technical knowledge to practical challenges.

Beyond my technical background, I have developed a strong passion for music and have worked as a professional music teacher for five years under a government project of the Ministry of Women and Children Affairs, Bangladesh. My musical achievements include becoming a District-Level Champion in a solo singing competition and earning multiple district-level championship titles through group performances. These experiences have enhanced my creativity, confidence, teamwork, and dedication to artistic expression.
